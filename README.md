# Donna K. Nkosi

**Business Analysis | Business Intelligence | Systems and Data Analysis**

I am an independent researcher developing a professional portfolio at the intersection of Business Analysis, Business Intelligence, data analysis and systems thinking.

My work is concerned with understanding how organisations operate, how information is produced and represented, how institutional processes affect outcomes, and how evidence can inform better decisions.

I am currently developing practical technical competencies in business analysis and business intelligence, applying these methods to a collection of empirical case studies and analytical projects.

## Areas of Interest

- **Business Analysis:** problem definition, stakeholder analysis, requirements elicitation, process analysis and solution design.
- **Business Intelligence:** data preparation, data modelling, SQL, data visualisation and performance reporting.
- **Systems Analysis:** organisational structures, information flows, institutional processes and operational relationships.
- **Research and Evidence:** data quality, information provenance, analytical methodology and evidence-based recommendations.
